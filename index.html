A small team building custom AI infrastructure.

Nexdim started as a studio for teams who needed AI systems built around their actual data and workflows — not another wrapper around a generic chatbot. Velmora, our voice AI platform, is the first product built on that infrastructure.

Every system we build — RAG pipeline, agent, or integration — is judged on one thing: whether it solves the problem it was built for. Everything else is in service of that.
